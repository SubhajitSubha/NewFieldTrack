# Everyday tasks, counted in taps

The test for the P round (ROADMAP section P): each everyday task should be reachable from Home in three taps or fewer,
not counting typing. Paths are as built on 2026-11-08. "Walked" means done on the emulator; the others are read off
the screens and their code. The old arrangement was not measured beforehand, so there is no before column.

## Over three taps, and what to do about it
- **Record money a farmer paid (4).** Also 3 by Customers tab, the farmer, Record payment; both ways are kept on
  purpose: the farmer's page is where people already go.
- **Change the language (4).** One more than the old Display screen, because the language is now a dropdown on
  Settings rather than chips on its own page. The dropdown lists nine languages, which pills cannot hold. Acceptable
  for a once-a-year choice; the language is also asked at the start.
- **See what a driver was paid (4), add a person (4), change what a driver may do (6).** Owner tasks done a few
  times a season. Could be shortened by a "Pay" button on each team card and by saving a role on the tap (no Save),
  if the owner wants; not done because a role change that saves on a tap is the one change on that page that
  cannot be taken back by the person it affects.
